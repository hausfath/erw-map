Feedstock grind

A physical assumption about the rock, not a preference. Specific surface area is the largest single uncertainty in any absolute CO₂ figure here, so it is a control rather than a hidden constant.

Economics

Off by default, so the landing map is a statement about physical potential. Economics adds a layer of assumption on top — a gate cost, a truck rate, a tortuosity factor, and a quarry inventory of uneven completeness — so it is something to switch on deliberately and switch off again.

Term sensitivity Physics

These are exponents on a physical product, not importance weights. At 1.00 the composite is gross CO₂ removal, so a location with no removal scores zero. Lower a term to ask what the map looks like if you distrust it — that is a what-if, not a preference.
